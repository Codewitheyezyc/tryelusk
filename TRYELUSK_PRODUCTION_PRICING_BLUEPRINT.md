### 📋 Cost Breakdown (Provider Cost for TryElusk):

#### 1. 🖼️ Phase 1: Pre-Production (Concept Art & Character DNA)
* **10 Locked Characters** $\times$ 3 turnaround angles (Front, Side, Close-up) = 30 images
* **30 Location & Environment Keyframes**
* **Total:** 60 GPT Image 2 stills (Medium 1080p @ $0.040/image)
* 💵 **Phase 1 Cost:** $60 \times \$0.040 =$ **$2.40**

---

#### 2. 🎬 Phase 2: Principal Video Production (Seedance 2.5)

You have 3 production pathways depending on resolution choices:

* **Route A: Pure 480p SD (with native audio):**
  * 200 video takes $\times$ $1.32 (6-second average take)
  * 💵 **Video Cost:** **$264.00**

* **Route B: Pure 720p HD (with native audio):**
  * 200 video takes $\times$ $2.80 (6-second average take)
  * 💵 **Video Cost:** **$560.00**

* **Route C: Hybrid Strategy (60 Hero Shots in 720p + 140 Shots in 480p):**
  * 60 Hero Takes in 720p ($168.00) + 140 Background/Action Takes in 480p ($184.80)
  * 💵 **Video Cost:** **$352.80**

---

#### 3. 🎙️ Phase 3: Post-Production (Dialogue, Voice & Lip-Sync)
* **60 Spoken Dialogue Lines** (Cinema Voice HD @ $0.03/track) = **$1.80**
